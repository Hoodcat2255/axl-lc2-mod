# Shooting on a rope keeps Axl drawn (user report 2026-11-05: shooting up / down on a rope he vanished): the rope aims
# of poses 2 / 3 had the PS1 anim numbers 229-232, which on PC are the wall aims and the Giga Crush pictures (bank B
# frame 216 drawn: nothing) - now anims 252-255 (axl_x.c ROPE_AIM, lc2_hook.ROPE_AIM_HI). The intro's level rope
# (pose 3, x 2368-2560, y 1008): forward, up-diagonal, down-diagonal, each 60 frames held; the picture is bank B 72 /
# 73 (texture 10, PAT section 18) and the shots go on (rapid fire).
all_weapons
seq
wait free == 1 9000
god on
ghost on
log rope_grab
goto 2440 1105 4
idle 20
hold UP Z
idle 40
hold UP
idle 20
release
idle 20
show p_0D anim px py
expect p_0D == 28 rope_on
log rope_forward
mark
press 30 X
snap
press 30 X
show bullets p_0D anim p_66
expect bullets >= 8 rope_forward_rapid
expect p_0D == 28 rope_forward_still_on
idle 30
log rope_up_diagonal
mark
press 30 X UP
show p_0D anim p_66 p_54 p_58
snap
expect anim == 254 rope_up_diagonal_anim
expect p_66 == 72 rope_up_diagonal_drawn
press 30 X UP
show bullets
expect bullets >= 8 rope_up_diagonal_rapid
idle 30
log rope_down_diagonal
mark
press 30 X DOWN
show p_0D anim p_66
snap
expect anim == 255 rope_down_diagonal_anim
expect p_66 == 73 rope_down_diagonal_drawn
press 30 X DOWN
show bullets
expect bullets >= 8 rope_down_diagonal_rapid
expect p_0D == 28 rope_still_on
idle 30
end
