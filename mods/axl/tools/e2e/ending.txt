# Sigma and the staff roll. The intro ends at once (its stage-end flag), the stage select is reached through the
# Hunter base (to_stage_select.inc), and at PLAYER SELECT the picked stage becomes 12 area 1 (Sigma's stage, the
# last of Gate's laboratory: checkpoints (160,224) and (3360,336)) - the game loads it as a picked stage, its tiles
# right (a checkpoint jump loads none). goto walks the corridor to Sigma's door through the free cells; Sigma's
# dialogues are paged with Z (he counts as free: page, not talk). Phase 1: the bot under god. Phase 2 (hit only when
# the mouth opens): hit points set to 1 once the bar is full. The ending's dialogues paged with Z only (Space would
# skip the roll), then the roll (stage 15 area 1) runs to its end, the Axl sprite credits on its last screens.
seq
include to_stage_select
set 48 C
set 49 1
press 4 Z
talk 6000 free == 1
expect stage == 12 sigma_stage
expect st_9A == 0 axl_not_falcon
god on
goto 3100 380 6
hold RIGHT
wait boss_alive == 1 1800
release
page 6000 boss_hp > 20
expect boss_hp > 20 sigma1_bar
bot boss
wait boss_hp == 0 9000
bot off
page 6000 boss_hp > 20
idle 300
expect boss_hp > 50 sigma2_bar_full
bset 84 1
bot boss
wait boss_hp == 0 9000
bot off
page 8000 stage == 11
page 20000 area == 1
expect stage == 15 roll_stage
expect area == 1 roll_area
# (the roll's frames: the Axl credits are on screen 13000-13400 frames after it starts, 2026-10-28 run sig5)
idle 13000
snap
idle 200
snap
expect area == 1 roll_running_at_axl_credits
idle 200
snap
end
