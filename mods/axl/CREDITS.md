## Axl sprite sheet

Axl's sprites, portraits, palettes (White Axl included) and the HUD / menu emblems come from the compilation
"Ultimate Axl Sprite Sheet (2021)" by JuggaloManX and others
(https://www.deviantart.com/juggalomanx/art/Ultimate-Axl-Sprite-Sheet-%282021%29-824018313; also on The Spriters
Resource as "Custom / Edited - Mega Man X Customs - Axl - Axl (PS1-Style, 2021 Update)"). The sheet asks to credit
all of the following:

- Capcom - for creating Mega Man
- megafan97 - sprite rips / sprite edition
- The Spriters Resource, Sprite Database, Sprites Inc. - sprite host websites
- JuggaloManX - color correcting / compilation
- DoriePiecrust - color correcting / sprite edition / compilation
- Yufforia - color correcting / compilation
- MasterChiefRamsay - compilation
- Busteromegamon, SlateDP - spriter / compilation
- Blackbeltdude, brongaa12, Commandmissionfan, FXFreitas, hansungkee, HeatTeamPro, Kensuyjin33, megaguy2222,
  NONE/Dragon, Rocklu, TheUltimateDark-Of, V-Nico - spriters
- and the sheet makers' friends on Discord who gave their thoughts and feedback

The same list closes X6's staff roll in the game (after EXECUTIVE PRODUCER).

## Game data

- Mega Man X6, Mega Man X8 and Mega Man X Legacy Collection 2: Capcom. Axl's voice and sound effects are taken
  from the X8 files that Legacy Collection 2 ships; the DNA Change bosses' looks, attacks and sounds from X6's own
  data.
- Axl's "Ready" at the stage start: a clip a tester made and gave for the mod (mods/axl/sound/axl_ready.ogg).
